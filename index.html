About Kalolsavam

A celebration of student creativity

School Kalolsavam is a vibrant platform where students can showcase their artistic talents and creativity. It brings together students from different schools and provides them with opportunities to participate in various cultural events.


From classical dance and music to drama, drawing and traditional performances, Kalolsavam celebrates the rich cultural spirit and artistic talents of students.
